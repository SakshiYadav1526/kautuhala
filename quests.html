The Quest Board

A quest is a project, experiment, or challenge that helps members explore a question and develop practical skills. Each quest begins with curiosity and grows through testing, collaboration, and learning.

The Quest Lifecycle

  1. Discover: Identify a question or problem worth exploring.
  2. Plan: Define the goal and divide the work into smaller tasks.
  3. Build: Develop a solution or experimental model.
  4. Test: Check whether the solution works as expected.
  5. Reflect: Document the results and identify what can be improved.

Types of Quests

Learning Quest
Focuses on understanding a new concept, language, or technology.
Building Quest
Focuses on creating a working website, application, tool, or prototype.
Research Quest
Focuses on investigating a question and explaining the findings clearly.
Collaboration Quest
Focuses on teamwork, code organisation, and combining different skills.

Quest Principles

  • Start with a clear question.
  • Choose realistic and measurable goals.
  • Keep the work organised and understandable.
  • Test your work instead of assuming it is correct.
  • Record problems, discoveries, and improvements.
  • Give credit to collaborators and external resources.
  • Share what you learned with the guild.
